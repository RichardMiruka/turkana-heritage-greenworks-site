Why this basket exists

Kwa nini kikapu hiki kipo

Weavers around Lake Turkana are skilled, but not paid for their skill.

Wafumaji wa eneo la Ziwa Turkana wana ujuzi, lakini hawalipwi kulingana na ujuzi wao.

  • 79.4% of Turkana residents live in poverty. The Kenyan average is 35%. ya wakazi wa Turkana wanaishi katika umaskini. Wastani wa Kenya ni 35%.
  • 20–74% of the Lodwar retail price is what weavers get, and they cannot set it. ya bei ya rejareja ya Lodwar ndiyo wanayopata wafumaji, na hawawezi kuipanga.
  • KSh 700 is what a weaver was paid for a basket in 2016. ndicho kilicholipwa kwa mfumaji kwa kikapu mwaka 2016.

Young people are not learning the craft because it does not pay. Doum palm, the raw material, needs managed harvesting that nobody funds.

Vijana hawajifunzi ufundi huu kwa sababu hauleti malipo. Mkoche, malighafi, unahitaji uvunaji unaosimamiwa ambao hakuna anayeufadhili.

Sources: Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS); IGAD study, 2016.

Vyanzo: Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS); utafiti wa IGAD, 2016.

From leaf to basket

Kutoka jani hadi kikapu

  1. Gathered

    Hukusanywa

    Doum palm leaves come from stands along the lake shore and the Turkwel River.

    Majani ya mkoche hutoka kwenye mikoche kando ya ufuo wa ziwa na Mto Turkwel.

  2. Coiled by hand

    Hufumwa kwa mikono

    A weaver splits and coils the leaf, to a community quality standard.

    Mfumaji hugawanya na kuviringa jani, kwa kiwango cha ubora cha jamii.

  3. Paid on delivery

    Hulipwa wakati wa kuwasilisha

    She delivers to the weaver-owned hub and is paid on delivery.

    Anawasilisha kwenye kituo kinachomilikiwa na wafumaji na analipwa mara anapowasilisha.

  4. Tagged

    Hutiwa lebo

    A QR tag links the basket to its maker, its palm stand and its planting record.

    Lebo ya QR inaunganisha kikapu na mfumaji wake, mikoche yake na rekodi yake ya upandaji.

  5. Yours

    Chako

    The basket reaches you, and this page shows where your money went.

    Kikapu kinakufikia, na ukurasa huu unaonyesha fedha yako ilikoenda.

What this sale plants

Kile mauzo haya yanapanda

10% of every sale funds replanting and protecting Doum palm stands.

Asilimia 10 ya kila mauzo hufadhili upandaji upya na ulinzi wa mikoche.

34

34 of 4,600 seedlings planted toward the year 1 target (sample count).

Miche 34 kati ya 4,600 imepandwa kuelekea lengo la mwaka wa 1 (hesabu ya mfano).

Our target: 70% or more of seedlings alive after 12 months, checked with KEFRI or KALRO. Results are published for every batch.

Lengo letu: miche 70% au zaidi iwe hai baada ya miezi 12, ikikaguliwa na KEFRI au KALRO. Matokeo huchapishwa kwa kila kundi la vikapu.

The community owns it

Jamii inaimiliki

The community owns its knowledge, its designs and the benefit-sharing. That cannot be copied from a catalogue.

Jamii inamiliki ujuzi wake, miundo yake na ugavi wa manufaa. Hilo haliwezi kunakiliwa kutoka kwenye katalogi.

  • Weavers set prices and share the surplus.
  • Wafumaji hupanga bei na kugawana ziada.
  • Weaving skills are held by women and elders, and recorded only with their consent.
  • Ujuzi wa kufuma unashikiliwa na wanawake na wazee, na hurekodiwa kwa ridhaa yao tu.
  • Free, prior and informed consent comes before any design is sold.
  • Ridhaa huru, ya awali na yenye taarifa kamili hutangulia kabla ya muundo wowote kuuzwa.
  • Regeneration results are published for every batch.
  • Matokeo ya urejeshaji wa mikoche huchapishwa kwa kila kundi.
